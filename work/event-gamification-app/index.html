Exhibitor Engagement Program

A booth-engagement web app for conference exhibitors: attendees scan a booth's QR code, answer a short quiz, and climb a live leaderboard, while organizers run the whole event from an admin console.

Role: SoloStatus: Live

  • React 19
  • TypeScript
  • Vite 7
  • Node.js + Express 5
  • PostgreSQL
  • PWA

Problem

At a conference like the Peptides & Complex Generics Symposium, attendees walk past exhibitor booths without much reason to stop and engage, and organizers have no easy way to see who actually visited which booth. This app turns a booth visit into a quick quiz and a point on a shared leaderboard, giving attendees a reason to go booth to booth and organizers a record of real engagement.

What I built

A PWA that attendees open by scanning a booth's QR code: they register once (name, mobile and/or email), get a signed session cookie, and from then on each new booth scan serves a two-question quiz drawn from that exhibitor's question pool, scored instantly and added to a live, event-wide leaderboard. Organizers get a separate admin console to manage exhibitors and their questions, generate and download booth QR codes, manage participants, and export everything as CSV or a JSON backup — with each event's exhibitors, participants and leaderboard fully isolated from every other event.

Key features

  • In-app QR scanner that launches a booth's quiz directly from the camera
  • Two random questions per booth from a pool of four or more, one attempt per booth, instant 10/5/0 scoring
  • A live, event-wide leaderboard
  • An admin console with full event isolation — exhibitors, participants and leaderboards don't cross between events
  • QR code generation per exhibitor, plus CSV and JSON exports for participants, quizzes and backups
  • Installable PWA with a network-first service worker for the app shell

The hardest part

Making sure nothing about a score or identity could be faked from the browser. The client never picks the quiz questions, scores an answer, or says who the participant is — the server does all three, reading identity from a signed, httpOnly cookie it issued itself. The one-attempt-per-booth rule is enforced by a Postgres unique constraint rather than client-side logic, so even two scans landing at the same instant can't both score. That meant designing the whole quiz flow server-authoritative from the start, rather than bolting on checks afterward.

Result

Built and deployed solo for exhibitor booths at the Peptides & Complex Generics Symposium 2026, where it performed well enough that it's now being folded into a broader event management platform rather than staying a standalone tool.
